QUESTIONS?

Everything you probably want to know before throwing something into the pond.

01 WHAT CAN I SUBMIT? +

Anything you made. Finished tracks, demos, remixes, rough ideas, weird experiments — if you think it's interesting, send it.

As long as it is your work, not copyrighted material you don't have permission to use, and not pornography.

02 CAN I SUBMIT UNFINISHED MUSIC? +

Yes.

Rough demos, unfinished tracks, sketches, strange experiments — all welcome. It doesn't have to be polished.

03 WHAT GENRES DO YOU ACCEPT? +

Any genre.

Ducki works across multiple genres, so there isn't a fixed genre requirement.

04 CAN I SUBMIT A REMIX? +

Yes, as long as you have the rights or permission required to use the original material.

05 HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE TO GET A RESPONSE? +

Fastest: 1–2 minutes.

Slowest: 1 week.

If there is no response after a week, it means your track has a problem.

06 DO I HAVE TO BE A PROFESSIONAL ARTIST? +

Nope.

If you made something interesting, that's enough.

07 HOW MANY THINGS CAN I SUBMIT? +

You can submit multiple times. Just make sure each submission has something worth sharing.

08 WILL YOU GIVE FEEDBACK? +

A submission is primarily a way to send something for consideration, not a guaranteed detailed feedback service.

09 IS THERE A SUBMISSION FEE? +

No.

10 WHY "DUCKI"? +

A duck is a flexible, multi-terrain animal. It can walk, swim, and fly.

That's basically the idea behind Ducki: adaptable, multi-genre, and not limited to one direction.

The "i" in Ducki stands for ideas.

A duck with ideas.
